# Expansion batch 02 v0.1

## Intake route

Candidates in this batch were identified through an RCSB PDB Search API query
for X-ray structures with reported resolution <= 2.0 Å and inhibitor-related
annotations.  The search result is only a discovery aid: eligibility is based
on the downloaded original-coordinate mmCIF inventory, not on the annotation.

| Case | PDB / component | Reported resolution | Inventory disposition |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| expansion-009 | 9CY0 / A1A55 | 0.8 Å | contextual: two chains and two ligand instances |
| expansion-010 | 6FMC / DUE | 0.9 Å | clean: one chain, one ligand instance, no other non-polymeric components |

## Boundaries

The first case remains pending a predeclared crystallographic-copy selection.
The second is represented separately in `CLEAN-BATCH-01-v0.1.md`; its strict
preparation outcome is reported there.  Neither discovery nor an apparent
structural simplicity is interpreted as a binding, potency, or docking-quality
claim.
